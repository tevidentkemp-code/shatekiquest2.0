HOW TO PLAY

  1. 1
    SPOT IT. TAP IT.

    Tap a square each time Steve says or does it. An orange square means you’ve collected it.

  2. 2
    PICK UP MINI WINS

    Make lines, collect the four corners or complete an X. These earn extra points while the game keeps going.

  3. 3
    FILL THE 4×4

    Collect all 16 main squares for a full house. The first full house wins and ends the game.

WINNING WAYS

♛

CHASE THE JACKPOT

Collect all 16 main squares, Word of the Day and all 3 Actions & Clothes tiles for 20 / 20. Collect the four extras before your final main square, which ends the game.

Tapped by mistake? Choose Remove, then tap the collected square once.
